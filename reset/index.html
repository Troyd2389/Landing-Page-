Why Reset?

Knowing What to Do Is Only Half the Problem.

Some people can take a plan and run with it. Others know they need more support changing their training, health behaviors, and consistency at the same time.

The Reset is for the second group: people who want a clear baseline and a more active coaching relationship over the next 12 weeks.

You want to get back in shape but have struggled to stay consistent.
You want your fitness plan connected to sleep, recovery, nutrition, stress, and lifestyle.
You want more accountability than an app alone provides.
You want both fitness and RN-led health and wellness coaching.
You want to reassess at the end and see measurable change.
