Start With a Baseline.

Most people know their weight and a few basic health numbers. That is not the same as knowing how healthy, fit, or physically capable they are.

You can't build the right plan if you don't know where you're starting.

How strong are you for the life you want to keep living?
How well do you move, balance, stabilize, and produce power?
How strong is your aerobic fitness and recovery?

The Relentless System

Assess → Understand → Improve → Reassess

The Reset gives you a clear starting point, a personalized path forward, and objective evidence of what changed.

1

Assess

Evaluate your physical capability and health behaviors through a comprehensive physical assessment and RN health assessment.

2

Understand

See where you're doing well, what needs attention, and which areas deserve priority instead of trying to improve everything at once.

What You Get

Your Relentless Reset Includes

Comprehensive Physical Assessment

Establish your baseline across strength, aerobic fitness, mobility, stability, balance, power, movement quality, and functional capability.

RN Health Assessment

Review health history, basic health markers and vitals, sleep, recovery, stress, nutrition habits, medications and supplements, lifestyle, and personal health goals.

Personalized 12-Week Plan

A clear strategy built around your assessment results, goals, lifestyle, and highest-priority areas.

Guided Fitness Coaching

Personalized training, exercise progression, technique guidance, strength and cardiovascular development, mobility, adjustments, and accountability.

RN-Led Health & Wellness Coaching

Practical health education, sleep and recovery support, nutrition and lifestyle guidance, behavior-change coaching, and ongoing accountability.

Progress Tracking & Reassessment

Track consistency and meaningful progress throughout the program, then reassess at 12 weeks so you can see what changed and what comes next.

Two Sides. One Plan.

Fitness + RN-Led Health Coaching

Relentless brings physical capability and health behavior coaching together so your plan reflects more than workouts alone.

Troy

Health & Fitness Coach

Troy leads the physical side of Relentless — assessing capability, developing personalized training, progressing strength and fitness, and helping clients build bodies that remain capable for life.

Liz, RN

RN Health & Wellness Coach

Liz brings her nursing background to the health side of Relentless — helping clients better understand their health history, lifestyle, basic health markers, recovery, nutrition habits, medications and supplements, and the behaviors that influence long-term health.

RN-led health and wellness coaching through Relentless focuses on health education, lifestyle and behavior change, accountability, and appropriate referral. It does not replace medical evaluation, diagnosis, or treatment from your healthcare provider.
