Common Questions

What People Want to Know Before Starting.

What does the assessment measure?

Strength, aerobic fitness, mobility, stability, balance, power, movement quality, and functional capability. Testing is interpreted in the context of your goals and current level.

What do I leave with?

Your Relentless Score, your highest-priority areas, a personalized 90-day action plan, app delivery, and a retesting point so you can see what changed.

Do I need to be in good shape first?

No. Relentless is for people starting over as well as experienced exercisers. The point is to establish your baseline, not prove how fit you already are.

Is this just personal training?

No. The assessment gives you clarity and direction first. You can then follow the plan with minimal support or choose Membership, Private Coaching, Reset, or separate in-person training when appropriate.

Where is Relentless available?

In-person assessments are currently offered in the Charleston, South Carolina area. Exact scheduling details are confirmed when you inquire.

What happens after 90 days?

We retest the important measures, compare results, update priorities, and decide what the next phase should focus on.
