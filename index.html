Common Questions

What People Want to Know Before Starting.

What does the assessment measure?

Strength, aerobic fitness, mobility, stability, balance, power, movement quality, and functional capability. The exact testing is selected and interpreted in context of your goals and current level.

What do I leave with?

Your Relentless Score, your highest-priority areas, a personalized 90-day action plan, and a clear retesting point so you can see what changed.

Do I need to be in good shape first?

No. Relentless is for people starting over as well as experienced exercisers. The point is to establish your baseline, not to prove how fit you already are.

Is this just personal training?

No. The assessment gives you clarity and direction first. You can then follow the plan with minimal ongoing support or choose a more guided Relentless service.

Where is Relentless available?

In-person assessments are offered in the Charleston, South Carolina area. Use the form below and we'll follow up with scheduling details.

What happens after 90 days?

We retest the important measures, compare your results, update your priorities, and decide what the next phase should focus on.
