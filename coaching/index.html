What You Get

High-Value Coaching Without High-Time Delivery.

Fully Customized Programming

Your training is built around your assessment, goals, schedule, equipment, and current capability rather than a generic template.

Weekly Asynchronous Review

Your coach reviews execution and relevant data without requiring a weekly meeting.

Monthly 20–30 Minute Coaching Call

Use a focused monthly conversation to review progress, solve problems, and refine the next phase.

Limited In-App Messaging

Ask focused questions and communicate about relevant training issues without creating an always-on coaching model.

Program Adjustments

Adapt training for travel, equipment changes, pain, limitations, schedule disruptions, and changing goals.

Wearable & Recovery Interpretation

When useful, incorporate wearable and recovery information into better training decisions.

Pain & Limitation Monitoring

Training is adjusted when symptoms or limitations affect what is appropriate, with referral out when something needs medical evaluation.

Periodic Retesting

Recheck the capabilities that matter, compare progress, and update priorities based on evidence.

In-person personal training sessions are not included and are priced separately when available.
