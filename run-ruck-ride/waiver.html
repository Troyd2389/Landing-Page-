Participant Agreement

Assumption of Risk, Release of Liability, and Emergency Authorization

Relentless Run • Ruck • Ride · Saturday, August 29, 2026 · Daniel Island Fitness, 145 River Landing Drive, Suite 101A, Daniel Island, SC 29492

Please read this agreement carefully. It affects legal rights. Registration is not complete unless the participant, or a parent or legal guardian for a participant under 18, accepts this agreement and provides an electronic signature.

1. Voluntary Participation and Fitness to Participate

I voluntarily choose to participate in the Relentless Run • Ruck • Ride event, including any run, walk, ruck, bicycle ride, warm-up, cool-down, gathering, route activity, or related event activity I select. I understand that participation is not required and that I may stop at any time.

I represent that I am physically and medically able to participate safely, or that I have obtained appropriate medical clearance. I am responsible for selecting an appropriate discipline, pace, distance, bicycle, footwear, equipment, and ruck load. I will not participate while impaired or when illness, injury, medication, weather, or another condition makes participation unsafe.

2. Acknowledgment and Assumption of Risk

I understand that running, walking, carrying weight, cycling, crossing public roads, and using sidewalks, boardwalks, trails, parks, shared-use paths, parking areas, and other public or private spaces involve inherent and other risks. These risks include, without limitation:

  • falls; collisions with vehicles, bicycles, pedestrians, animals, fixed objects, or other participants;
  • traffic, road crossings, uneven ground, roots, gravel, sand, curbs, wet or slippery surfaces, narrow paths, blind turns, and changing route conditions;
  • heat, humidity, sun exposure, storms, lightning, dehydration, overexertion, fatigue, dizziness, cardiac events, respiratory distress, and other illness;
  • improperly fitted, defective, or failing bicycles, helmets, packs, weighted vests, footwear, or other equipment;
  • acts or omissions of participants, volunteers, motorists, members of the public, property owners, event personnel, and others; and
  • serious bodily injury, permanent disability, paralysis, death, emotional distress, property loss, and property damage.

I knowingly and voluntarily accept and assume all risks of participation, whether known or unknown, foreseeable or unforeseeable, and whether arising from the nature of the activity, route conditions, my own conduct, the conduct of others, or the ordinary negligence of a Released Party identified below.

3. Release and Waiver of Liability

To the fullest extent permitted by South Carolina law, I release, waive, discharge, and covenant not to sue Relentless; Longevity Performance Group; Daniel Island Fitness; the event sponsors and community partners identified on the event website or event-day materials; event volunteers and route-support personnel; property owners and property managers whose premises are used in connection with the event; and each of their respective owners, officers, directors, employees, agents, contractors, representatives, successors, and assigns (collectively, the “Released Parties”) from any and all claims, demands, causes of action, damages, losses, liabilities, costs, or expenses arising out of or related to my participation, including claims based on the ordinary negligence of a Released Party.

This release does not apply to gross negligence, reckless or willful misconduct, intentional misconduct, or any claim that cannot legally be released or waived.

4. Responsibility for My Conduct; Limited Indemnification

I agree to follow event rules, route instructions, traffic laws, posted signs, and reasonable directions from event personnel. I will use equipment in safe working condition, wear a properly fitted helmet for the bicycle portion, yield to pedestrians, ride conservatively, and avoid racing or aggressive passing during the ride.

To the fullest extent permitted by law, I agree to defend, indemnify, and hold harmless the Released Parties from third-party claims, losses, or expenses caused by my own negligent, reckless, or intentional acts, my violation of event rules or traffic laws, or my unsafe or unauthorized use of equipment.

5. Emergency Care and Medical Costs

If I am injured or become ill and cannot make decisions for myself, I authorize event personnel and others to contact emergency services and to provide or arrange reasonable first aid, emergency transportation, and medical treatment. I understand that event personnel may not be medical professionals and that emergency assistance cannot be guaranteed. I accept financial responsibility for medical care, transportation, and related expenses incurred on my behalf.

6. Personal Property

I am responsible for my bicycle, helmet, pack, weights, phone, vehicle, and all other personal property. I release the Released Parties from claims for loss, theft, or damage to personal property except to the extent a claim cannot legally be waived.

7. Minor Participants

If the participant is under 18, I certify that I am the participant’s parent or legal guardian and have authority to enter this agreement. I consent to the minor’s participation, accept the risks described above, authorize emergency care, and agree to this agreement on my own behalf and, to the fullest extent permitted by law, on behalf of the minor. I accept responsibility for selecting activities and equipment appropriate for the minor and for ensuring appropriate supervision.

8. Governing Law, Severability, and Entire Agreement

This agreement is governed by the laws of the State of South Carolina. If any provision is held invalid or unenforceable, it will be limited or severed to the minimum extent necessary, and the remaining provisions will remain in effect. This document contains the entire agreement concerning assumption of risk, release of liability, emergency authorization, and related matters for this event and may be modified only in a written agreement signed by the participant or signer and an authorized representative of Relentless.

9. Electronic Signature and Acknowledgment

By checking the required waiver box, typing my full legal name, and submitting the registration form, I intend to provide an electronic signature. I acknowledge that I have had the opportunity to read this agreement, understand its terms, understand that I am giving up legal rights including certain claims based on ordinary negligence, and sign voluntarily without coercion.
