The Relentless Founding Format

Everyone completes the same three disciplines—in the same order.

This is a community challenge, not an elite-only race. Move at your level, complete the full format, and establish your first Relentless benchmark.

Run

5 kilometers · 3.1 miles

Run, jog, or walk the full course. Participants who want a benchmark may record their time.

Goal: Move with purpose and finish the full distance.

Ruck

1 mile · Self-selected load

Carry a secure backpack or ruck for one mile. There is no required minimum weight for the founding event.

Suggested load: About 5–10% of body weight for newer participants, 10–15% for experienced ruckers, and up to 20% only if you already train safely with that load.

Ride

5 kilometers · Scenic ride

The ride is required for completion but is intentionally untimed. It is a scenic, leisure-paced finish on the designated shared-use route.

No e-bikes. Helmets are mandatory.

Founding-event principle: Run and ruck at your chosen pace. Ride with control, respect the shared trail, and return safely to the community finish.

Safety Standard

The ride is scenic—not a race.

Daniel Island’s paths are shared with walkers, families, runners, and other cyclists. Every participant is responsible for riding conservatively and following the event rules.

  • Wear a properly fitted helmet for the entire ride.
  • No e-bikes, scooters, or motorized vehicles.
  • Yield to pedestrians at all times.
  • Slow down at crossings, blind turns, narrow sections, and congested areas.
  • Pass only when there is ample room and clearly announce, “Passing on your left.”
  • No drafting, racing, aggressive passing, or headphones during the ride.
  • Follow all route signs, traffic controls, and instructions from event staff.
  • Unsafe riding may result in removal from the event.

What to Bring

Arrive ready.

  • A bicycle in safe working condition
  • A properly fitted bike helmet
  • A secure backpack or ruck
  • Your chosen ruck weight
  • Water and electrolytes
  • Athletic shoes and weather-appropriate clothing
  • Sunscreen and a positive attitude

Frequently Asked Questions

Know before you go.

Do I have to complete all three disciplines?

Yes. Official founding-event completion requires the 5K run, 1-mile ruck, and 5K scenic ride in that order. You may move at the pace appropriate for you.

Is this a race?

The event can provide a personal benchmark, but the core experience is community and completion. The bike segment is untimed and must not be raced.

How much weight should I carry?

Ruck weight is self-selected. Roughly 5–10% of body weight is a reasonable starting range for newer participants. Experienced ruckers may choose 10–15%, while 20% should be reserved for people already conditioned to that load.

Can I use an e-bike?

No. The founding event is limited to non-electric bicycles.

Can beginners participate?

Yes. You may run, jog, or walk. Choose a manageable ruck load and ride conservatively. You should still be able to safely complete the total event volume.

What happens if the weather is unsafe?

Safety updates, postponement information, or revised instructions will be sent to the email and phone number used during registration.

Are active Relentless clients really free?

Yes. Participation is included for active Relentless coaching clients. Select the client registration type and your status will be verified.
